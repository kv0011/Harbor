How it works

Three steps. No account, no waiting.

01

Paste a link

Drop in any URL you weren't expecting — an email link, a text message, a QR code destination.

02

We run 13 checks

Host type, domain age, redirect chains, shortener services, structural red flags — each one scored independently.

03

Get a verdict, with reasons

Not just "safe" or "unsafe" — the exact signals that drove the call, so you can judge the judgment.

Detection signals

What Harbor actually looks at.

No single check is decisive on its own. Together, they build a picture.

HOST_IP

Host & IP checks

Flags raw IP addresses standing in for a domain name, and hosts hidden behind an "@" trick.

DOMAIN_AGE

Registration & age

Freshly registered domains are disproportionately used for short-lived phishing campaigns.

REDIRECTS

Redirects & shorteners

Traces shortened links and redirect chains back to where they actually lead.

STRUCTURE

URL structure

Length, subdomain depth, and hyphen-padded brand names ("paypal-secure-login") — all measurable, all meaningful.

DNS

DNS & network signals

A domain with no resolvable DNS record is a domain that shouldn't be trusted yet.

MODEL

Weighted verdict

A trained model weighs all 13 signals together — it's rare that any one check decides the outcome alone.

Security & privacy

A link checker you don't have to check up on.

Handing over a suspicious link means trusting us with it, however briefly. Here's exactly what we do and don't do with it.

  • Links aren't stored after checking

    A URL you submit is analyzed and discarded. We keep aggregate pass/fail counts, never the link itself.

  • No third-party ad trackers

    Nothing you check here is shared with advertisers or data brokers.

  • Methodology is published, not a black box

    Every signal Harbor checks is documented — you can see exactly why a verdict came out the way it did.
